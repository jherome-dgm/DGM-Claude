Digital Group Media Limited

Whilst we were incorporated in 2016 the business was started by Managing Director James Middleditch back in 2008 which then became a partnership in 2012 when Danny joined him and the team has grown from there to include Nick, Mark, Jherome, Jemma and a growing team of other dedicated digital marketing specialists that we work with exclusively to fulfil aspects of our client projects such as Google Ads Management, SEO, Copywriting etc.

We’re headquartered at our central office in the heart of Birmingham’s creative quarter in Digbeth at the legendary Custard Factory.

Whilst we have our own permanent office, we’ve been leveraging remote working since long before the pandemic. This means we can use the best people and they have the freedom to work in the environment that is most conducive to their happiness and ultimately the quality of their work. This is why we offer often unbeatable value as we simply don’t carry the overhead costs that many other web design & digital marketing agencies do.

We’re proud to be small & flexible and don’t pretend to be anything but. This means that every project gets top-level involvement on our part and no client gets fobbed off with the office junior or apprentice.

We’d be more than happy to have a chat on in-person, on zoom or over the phone about how we might be able to help you with your web design & digital marketing needs.

Our Values

  • Respect – We have tremendous respect for our clients and the businesses that they’re growing. It’s hard work and not for the faint hearted. We also respect ourselves, so it’s a two-way street which leads to great mutual partnerships.
  • Knowledge – Our ability to make a living is dependant on how effectively we can produce results. We attack our knowledge gaps with gusto and continue to spend thousands on developing our capabilities in all disciplines.
  • Meaningful Results – We only work on projects where all the people involved are dependant on positive outcomes and meaningful results. It keeps everyone focused on moving forward and the buzz you get when a specific and measurable result is achieved is hard to beat. It may seem like stating the obvious but it’s a nightmare working with unaccountable people that aren’t invested in meaningful results even if they have the budget to spend.
  • Transparency – We like to be open about what we do, how we do it and what the strengths & weaknesses of any trajectory are. Digital Marketing can be a scary world for the uninitiated, and we prefer to cut through the BS.

These are the 10 key activity areas that businesses depend on if they want to see growth or stable revenue through a regular flow of qualified inbound sales enquiries. Each element has a bearing on the other and the importance of each element will vary from one business to another. You may need to work on one, some, or all of these to unlock the results you deserve.

Foundation

These are the fundamentals that if understood with clarity make everything else in sales & marketing far easier & more effective.

Brand & Positioning

This is about more than having a nice logo, this is about how people perceive your brand, your value proposition and where it fits in the marketplace.

Website

The hub of most of your sales & marketing activity & the primary source of information for your prospects. Your website should be your best salesperson.

Search & AI Discoverability

This is about understanding the opportunities that exist on the search engines & AI platforms. Can people find you when searching for a solution to their problem or need?

Content Marketing

How effectively or strategically are you using video, images, audio & text to become the trusted thought leader in your market?

Email

We all send & receive email, some more effectively than others. This relatively low cost tool enables you to automate and scale the delivery of your value proposition to the right people at the right time.

Social Media

Whatever your personal preferences, most of your customers are using some combination of Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn & Twitter etc. Are you part of the conversation?

Offline Marketing

How effectively are you using time tested traditional sales & marketing methods to generate leads & sales?

Insights & Analytics

What gets measured gets improved. Tangible success is difficult to replicate or scale unless you measure and improve on how it occurs in the first place.

Coaching & Consultancy
