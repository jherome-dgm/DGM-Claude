Why Google Ads?

Get to The Top of Google

Google Ads gives your business direct access to appear at the top of search engine results pages (SERPs) for relevant searches. This prime position increases your online visibility and maximises the chances of users clicking on your ads.

Beat Your Competition

Google Ads provides you with the tools to stay ahead and capture the attention of your potential customers other competitors get a chance to, however good their SEO. With great ads and targetting you can dominate.

Laser-Targeted Advertising

Precisely target your ads to reach the most relevant audience based on factors such as location, demographics, interests, and search intent. This precision ensures that your ad budget is spent efficiently on potential customers.

Instant Results

Unlike many traditional marketing channels, Google Ads can deliver nearly instant results. Your ads can start generating traffic and leads as soon as your campaign is live, providing a rapid boost to your online presence.

Measurable ROI

Google Ads offers robust tracking and analytics tools, allowing you to measure the return on investment (ROI) of your campaigns accurately. You can see which keywords, ads, and strategies are delivering the best results and adjust your approach accordingly.

Control & Flexibility

Google Ads gives you the flexibility to adapt quickly. You can adjust your ad spend, scale campaigns up or down, pause or resume them based on your business needs and market dynamics. Whether you're responding to seasonal trends, budget changes, or shifting business priorities.

Who This Is NOT For?

Risk-Adverse Business Owners

If you prefer conservative, low-risk marketing approaches and are uncomfortable with the inherent experimentation and adaptability required for successful Google Ads campaigns, this platform may not be the best fit for your business.

Budget-Constrained Businesses

Google Ads can be cost-effective, but it may not be suitable for businesses with extremely limited marketing budgets, as it requires a reasonable investment to see significant results. We can advise you on budgets.

Short-Term Marketing Goals

If your business is solely focused on short-term objectives and is not willing to commit to a longer-term advertising strategy, Google Ads may not align with your goals.

Lack of Clear Value Proposition

If your business struggles to articulate a clear value proposition or unique selling points, Google Ads may not be effective in driving conversions, as it relies on compelling offers that buyers want to engage with.

Unwillingness to Adapt or Feedback

Google Ads campaigns require ongoing adjustments and optimisations. If your business is not open to adapting strategies based on data and insights, it may not benefit fully from this platform.

Poor Web Presence

A Google Ad is essentially selling a user on clicking through to see more on your website and if your website is poor or perhaps simply not as good as the competition's, even the best optimised campaign in the world will struggle.

How it works

Step 1:

Discovery Call

Start off by hopping on a Zoom call with one of the Directors (James or Danny) where we'll…

  • Talk about your business to get an understanding of its USPs, products, services and your target audience.
  • Take a look at some numbers and explore how much you should reasonably expect to invest to acquire new leads & customers.
  • Share some customised insights about your relevant search terms (keywords), search volumes, cost per click, buying intent, competitor's ads etc
  • Sense check your objectives & budgets against Google's data to determine what needs to happen for any campaign to be a clear success and how much risk (if any) is likely to be involved.
  • Model a number of potential outcomes (best and worst case scenarios) to ensure that your advertising goals align with achievable results.

These Zoom discovery calls take about 30mins and we put a lot of effort into preparing for them in advance in order to give you as much quality info as possible so that you can make informed decisions. There's never any obligation or expectation from us for you to do anything. Either way, you'll come away from the call with a competitive advantage (as well as the call recording and our proprietary keyword research).

Step 2:

Onboarding & Set-up

If it makes clear business sense for you to try Google Ads with us, we'll get the ball rolling and…

  • Send your initial invoice to secure your slot, marking the formal start of our partnership.
  • Confirm objectives, budgets, and target locations.
  • We'll review and if feasible improve your landing pages.
  • Analyse competitors to ensure we understand the competitive landscape.
  • Select the best campaign and bidding strategies to suit your business.
  • Get to work on building out engaging and effective ad creative, designed to capture attention and drive action.
  • We'll assist with adding your billing details and getting you direct access to the Google Ads platform.
  • Get access to (or create if you don't have them) your Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics accounts. This allows us to set up conversion tracking so we know what's happening after someone clicks an ad.
  • Install advanced call and form tracking, along with Hotjar, which provides us with deeper insights into user behaviour and campaign effectiveness.
  • We'll handle any necessary account verification processes to ensure compliance and readiness.

This onboarding and setup process usually takes between 1 to 2 weeks, depending on the specific needs and complexity of your account. Our goal is to ensure that everything is aligned perfectly with your objectives and that we give your account the best push off the side that we can.

Step 3:

Campaign Launch & Monitoring

As we launch your Google Ads campaigns, our attention turns to fine-tuning and performance monitoring:

  • We begin real-time monitoring and adjustment to manage normal performance fluctuations effectively.
  • Regular updates are provided as necessary (ahead of moving onto more scheduled reporting), balancing informed communication with focused optimisation.
  • In case of no or few impressions or clicks, we swiftly adjust bids, targeting, and ensure ad compliance to rectify any issues.
  • Budget management is key; we optimise spending, ensuring it aligns with search volumes and targeting strategies.
  • Fluctuations in metrics like CPC (Cost Per Click) and CTR (Click Through Rate) are normal; our focus remains on achieving your strategic goals rather than chasing transient numbers.
  • Our approach is patient and data-driven, avoiding knee-jerk reactions to short-term trends to ensure long-term campaign success.

This phase is about establishing a solid foundation for your campaigns, with our team proactively managing and optimising every aspect to deliver the best possible results.

Step 4:

Monitor, Optimise & Report

This step encapsulates the vital ongoing management and optimisation of your Google Ads campaigns, where continuous improvement is our mantra:

  • If eligible (and so far all Google Ads 1st timers have been) your £400 ad credit get applied within the first 60 days.
  • Regular checks are a staple of our routine, including monitoring conversion actions, assessing quality scores, and evaluating Google's recommendations discerningly.
  • We keep a close eye on search impression share and demographic performance, refining target audiences and adding negative keywords to enhance campaign relevance and efficiency.
  • Analysis of search term reports and audience responses informs our strategy, ensuring we're targeting the most responsive and valuable segments.
  • We constantly evaluate the performance of ads, keywords, and landing pages, seeking out the top performers and optimising accordingly.
  • Listening to calls generated from ads and reviewing form submissions, we assess lead quality, providing insights into campaign effectiveness.
  • HotJar data is scrutinised to understand user behaviour on your landing pages, revealing opportunities for further optimisation.
  • Proactively looking for opportunities, we test new campaigns, ads, and creative approaches to keep your strategy fresh and effective.
  • Our reporting goes beyond automated numbers; we provide human insights and interpretations, explaining key metrics, our actions, future strategies, and the reasoning behind them.

In this stage, our goal is to not only maintain but elevate the performance of your campaigns, ensuring that every aspect is continuously refined for maximum impact and alignment with your evolving business objectives.
